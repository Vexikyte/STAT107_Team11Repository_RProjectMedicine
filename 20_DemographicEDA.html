Age Distribution

mean(clean_data$age_years, na.rm = TRUE)
## [1] 43.83303
median(clean_data$age_years, na.rm = TRUE)
## [1] 44
plot_histogram(clean_data, "age_years", "Age (years)")

The participants are between 20 and 69 years old, and their average/meann age is about 43.83 years while our median age is 44. The histogram helps us see the age ranges of our participants.

BMI Distribution

mean(clean_data$bmi, na.rm = TRUE)
## [1] 29.28915
median(clean_data$bmi, na.rm = TRUE)
## [1] 28.19
plot_histogram(clean_data, "bmi", "BMI")

The average/mean BMI is about 29.29 while our median is 28.19. Our histogram shows the BMI Distribution of our participants, the frequency of participants in each BMI range. We can clearly see how this historgram is skewed right.

Summary

These graphs give us a background overview of the participants in our dataset. They show how age, BMI, sex, and race/ethnicity are distributed before we compare reported back pain with spinal examination measurements in 21_EDA.Rmd.
